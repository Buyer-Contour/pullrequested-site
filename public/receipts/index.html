The three accepted rows

Prices and availability are historical observations, not live quotes. Audience notes are inferences. “Last checked” refers to the checks for the original delivery, not the date of the underlying event.

Milo (Plazmi)

  • Product and inferred audience: A programmable Ledger Bell and a greenhouse design in the maker catalog. Inferred audience: Plazmi neighbors who build or visit interactive homes. Last checked: 2026-11-26.
  • Price observed 2026-11-26: Undisclosed. No public listing price for either item in the public shop data; this does not establish that either is free. Last checked: 2026-11-26.
  • Dated evidence: The house record dates the bell’s placement to 2026-10-18. Last checked: 2026-11-26.
  • The house log records a visitor ringing it on 2026-11-15. Placement and use are documented; a commercial launch was not verified in these records. Last checked: 2026-11-26.
  • Backers: Undisclosed. The maker catalog, house and shop records, and a public press and funding search for Milo and Plazmi did not identify a backer or a legal entity to match to a filing. That is not proof of no backing. Last checked: 2026-11-26.

Botto

  • Product and inferred audience: Community-selected digital artworks minted and auctioned by a decentralized autonomous artist. Inferred buyers: collectors of AI and digital art. Last checked: 2026-11-26.
  • Price observed 2026-11-26: Auction-based; current price undisclosed. The official auction page showed no live auction and no current price to quote. Last checked: 2026-11-26.
  • Dated evidence: Botto’s period notes date the start of Borrowed Light to 2026-10-06 and the start of Botto naming weekly winners to 2026-10-12. Last checked: 2026-11-26.
  • Backers: BottoDAO disclosed on 2023-10-25 that Variant Fund acquired 3.75 million BOTTO in an over-the-counter swap for USDC. This was a strategic token purchase, not an equity round. Last checked: 2026-11-26.

Infinite Objects

  • Product and inferred audience: Physical Video Prints. The wall-mounted XXL Square Wood option can display custom art or use White Walls. Inferred buyers: digital-art collectors looking to display work at home or in a gallery. Last checked: 2026-11-26.
  • Price observed 2026-11-26: US$1,399 for that model. The store data showed it available. Last checked: 2026-11-26.
  • Dated evidence: The store data dates the XXL listing to 2024-11-18. Last checked: 2026-11-26.
  • A Winter 2025 availability notice said acrylic units were out of stock. Exact notice date: undisclosed in the notice. No newer launch verified in the product or store sources. Last checked: 2026-11-26.
  • Backers: Courtside VC’s portfolio identifies its seed investment. Reporting dated 2021-05-27 names Courtside as lead and Dapper Labs and Serena Ventures among the participants in the $6 million seed round. Last checked: 2026-11-26.
